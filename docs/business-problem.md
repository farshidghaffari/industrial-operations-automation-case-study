# Business Problem

Many small and medium-sized industrial businesses still rely on manual processes to manage daily operations and financial records.

These manual processes can work at first, but they become harder to control when the business grows or when more people are involved.

## Common Problems

The business workflow included several areas that needed better structure:

- Daily operational records were not centralized
- Sales and invoices needed better tracking
- Customer debts needed clearer visibility
- Supplier debts needed reporting
- Purchases needed to be connected to financial status
- Checks needed a dedicated management workflow
- Expenses needed categorization and date filtering
- Employee information needed a structured record
- Financial account movements needed a ledger-style history
- Reports needed to be easier to access

## Why Manual Tracking Is Risky

Manual or scattered tracking can create problems such as:

- Repeated data entry
- Missing records
- Difficult reporting
- Hard-to-track debts
- Unclear financial movements
- More human error
- Slow decision-making
- Limited auditability

## Automation Goal

The goal was not simply to create forms and tables.

The real goal was to transform the business workflow into a system that supports:

- Better visibility
- Better control
- Better reporting
- Easier debugging of operational issues
- More reliable financial tracking
- Future expandability
