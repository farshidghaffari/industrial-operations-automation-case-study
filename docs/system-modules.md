# System Modules

This page preserves the module scope described in the earlier public documentation. It is not a fresh module-by-module verification of the current production release. The owner confirms that the overall system was delivered and used operationally, with ongoing supervision and iterative improvements. See the [evidence register](evidence-register.md) for current-source limitations.

The descriptions below should be read as previously documented functionality; they do not imply that every module remains under active development.

## 1. Daily Operations

Records daily operational activity and helps connect operational records with sales and reporting.

## 2. Sales and Invoices

Manages invoices, invoice items, payments, and related customer balances.

Key focus areas:

- Invoice creation
- Invoice display
- Payment tracking
- Customer receivables
- Daily sales reporting

## 3. Purchases

Records items purchased by the business, purchase prices, suppliers, settlement status, and payment details.

Key focus areas:

- Supplier selection
- Purchase items
- Quantity and unit price
- Paid amount
- Remaining balance
- Purchase settlement status

## 4. Customer Debts

Tracks unpaid customer balances and shows receivables in a clear and filtered way.

## 5. Supplier Debts

Tracks amounts owed to suppliers and supports supplier-level debt reporting.

## 6. Check Management

Tracks received checks, issued checks, due dates, amounts, statuses, and transfer flows.

Key focus areas:

- Received checks
- Issued checks
- Due date tracking
- Check status
- Check transfer
- Selecting registered checks in payment workflows

## 7. Expenses

Records business expenses and supports date filtering and categorization.

## 8. Employees

Stores employee information in a structured way.

Examples of information:

- Name
- National ID
- Insurance number
- Contact details
- Emergency contact
- Blood group
- Payment records

## 9. Financial Accounts

Tracks account activity in a ledger-style structure.

Examples:

- Cash accounts
- Card reader accounts
- Bank or transfer accounts
- Account transactions
- Financial history

## 10. Reports and Dashboard

Summarizes important business metrics and operational information.

Examples:

- Monthly sales
- Receivables
- Recent activity
- Financial account movements
- Debts
- Operational summaries
